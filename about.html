About A scenography and event production studio built on
one conviction: that space is the most powerful medium in any event.

The Studio

Sans Titre is a Beirut-based scenography and creative production studio, founded in 2026. We design and produce fashion shows, brand experiences, and special events for luxury clients across the region and beyond.

The studio brings together scenographers, producers, and technical specialists who share a single conviction: that the spaces we build carry meaning, and that every event deserves to be a singular experience.

Fashion Shows Sans Titre gives a designer's vision physical form, from spatial concept to the final bow.
Brand Experiences and Special Events We craft memorable events and experiences that leave a lasting visual and emotional imprint.
