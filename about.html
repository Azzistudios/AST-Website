The Studio

Sans Titre is a Beirut-based scenography and creative production studio, founded in 2026 by Marc Azzi, architect and scenographer. We design and produce special events, brand experiences, and fashion shows for luxury clients across the region and beyond.

Trained as an architect, Marc Azzi leads each project himself, from the first drawing to the last night.

The studio stays deliberately independent: each project is led hands-on, from first concept to final build, with the producers and technical specialists a brief calls for brought in as needed. One conviction runs through all of it: that the spaces we build carry meaning, and that every event deserves to be a singular experience.

The work sits in fashion, jewellery, beauty, spirits, automotive, art and culture.

How we work

Concept. We read the brief, the venue, and the intention, and answer with a spatial idea.
Design. The idea is drawn, modelled, and costed until it holds.
Build. We produce it on site: fabrication, lighting, technical, and the final call.
