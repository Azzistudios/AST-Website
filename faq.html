Frequently asked questions

What does Atelier Sans Titre do?

We are a scenography and event production studio in Beirut. We design and produce special events, brand experiences and fashion shows, from the first spatial concept to the final execution.

What is a scenography studio, and how is it different from an event agency?

An event agency coordinates logistics. A scenography studio designs the space itself: the architecture, atmosphere and staging that carry the meaning of the event. We do both: creative authorship and its production.

Do you only design, or do you also produce and handle technical execution?

Both. We take a project from concept and scenographic design through fabrication, staging, lighting and show-calling. A single team accountable for the whole space.

Where do you work? Do you travel outside Lebanon?

We are based in Beirut and work across Lebanon, the Gulf, Europe and wherever a project takes us. Travel and international production are part of how we work.

What sectors do you work with?

Fashion, jewellery, beauty, spirits, automotive, and culture and the arts.

How much does it cost to produce a fashion show or event?

Every project is scoped to its ambition, scale and venue, so there is no fixed price. Share your date, location and intention and we will prepare a considered proposal.

How far in advance should we book you?

Earlier is better. The strongest work comes from time to design. For a fashion show or a large event, a few months' lead is ideal. We also take on tighter timelines when the brief allows.

Can you work alongside our existing brand or creative team?

Yes. We collaborate closely with in-house teams and creative directors, and because we are independent, you work directly with ours. No layers, no group overhead.

What information do you need to prepare a proposal?

The occasion, the date, an approximate guest count, the venue or city, and any creative direction you already hold. The more you share, the sharper the response.

Do you handle venue, fabrication, lighting and staging?

Yes. Scenographic design plus the production that realises it: venue treatment, set fabrication, lighting, technical and on-site direction.

Are you part of Azzi Studios?

Atelier Sans Titre is part of the Azzi Studios creative ecosystem, and operates as its own studio with its own identity and creative direction.

What makes Atelier Sans Titre different?

Independent creative authorship, a direct line to the creative director, and cultural intelligence from inside the region. Not imported. Space is our medium.

How do we start a project?

Write to contact@ateliersanstitre.com or call +961 78 90 26 28 with a few lines about your event. We will take it from there.

Tell us the occasion.

A date, a place, an intention. We will answer with a space.
