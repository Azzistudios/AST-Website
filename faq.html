Frequently asked questions

What does Atelier Sans Titre do?

We are an event scenography studio in Beirut. We design the room an event happens in: the way in, the tables, the light. For weddings and private events, brand and corporate events, and fashion shows. Then we plan it, build it and run it on the day.

What is event scenography, and how is it different from décor?

Décor dresses a room. Scenography designs the space itself: how guests arrive, where they sit, where they look, how the light changes as the night goes on. We design first, then produce what we design.

Are you wedding planners?

We are wedding designers who also plan. The design comes first: the concept, the spaces, the light. Then we plan the day beneath it, coordinate the suppliers and run it on the day. One studio, from the first coffee to the last guest.

What do you design for private clients?

Weddings, engagements, private dinners, birthdays and parties.

What do you design for brands?

Fashion shows, launches, activations, pop-ups, press days, window displays, corporate dinners, galas and vernissages.

Where do you work?

Lebanon first: Beirut, Mount Lebanon, the mountains and the coast. We travel to the region and to Europe when a project calls for it, including weddings for Lebanese families marrying abroad.

We live abroad and are coming home to marry. Can you help?

Yes. We are here all year. We walk the venues for you, at the hour you will use them, and send you the drawings. You meet us in person when you land.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted as one all-in figure, built from the spaces we design, what is built and the days on site. You see the breakdown underneath: design, production, coordination. Tell us your date, place, guest count and budget, and we send one proposal.

How far ahead should we book?

Weddings: 9 to 12 months, 12 to 18 if you are planning from abroad. Brand events and fashion shows: two to three months is ideal. We take shorter timelines when the brief allows.

How do payments work?

50% at signing, which holds your date. 40% sixty days before the event. 10% seven days before. In US dollars.

What if we need to postpone?

Your date can move once, within 18 months, and your deposit carries over. A new date more than 12 months away is quoted at that year's rates.

Do you handle production and suppliers?

Yes: fabrication, rentals, flowers, lighting, sound and the team on site. Suppliers are chosen for the design and coordinated by us.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Yes. We collaborate closely with in-house teams and creative directors, and because we are independent, you work directly with ours. No layers, no group overhead.

Are you part of Azzi Studios?

Atelier Sans Titre is part of the Azzi Studios creative ecosystem and works as its own studio, with its own identity and creative direction.
